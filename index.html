03 — The Shop

Signature Suits, in the heart of Bulawayo.

We specialise in high-quality, affordable suits tailored for men, women and kids — perfect for weddings, formal events, business meetings and special occasions.

Our suits are crafted in Turkey, and we carry a wide range of stylish, modern designs alongside classic, timeless styles. Whether you are after a custom-fit suit or a ready-to-wear option, you will find it here.

You will find us at 90 on George Square Mall, on the first floor, opposite Watering Hole, along George Silundika Avenue between 8th and 9th Avenue. We pride ourselves on exceptional customer service and a commitment to helping you look your best.
